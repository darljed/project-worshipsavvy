# WorshipSavvy

**Put the Word on screen.**

Church presentation software for song lyrics, Bible verses, sermons and media —
built for real Sunday services on both **macOS and Windows**, and controlled
from any phone or tablet on the church Wi-Fi. An answer to EasyWorship.

## What is this repository?

This is the **public home for WorshipSavvy** — where the app is released,
where users report issues and request features, and where the community
gathers. The application source code lives in a separate repository; **this
repo is the hub for:**

- **📦 Releases & downloads** — installers for macOS and Windows, published on

  or support.

## What WorshipSavvy does

| Area | What your team gets |
|---|---|
| 🎵 **Songs** | Paste a song once — it's split into clean slides. Stores CCLI number, authors, copyright. Full-text search by title or lyric. |
| 📖 **Bible** | Built-in and offline: **KJV, ASV, WEB** (English) and **Ang Dating Biblia 1905** (Tagalog). Look up a verse, a passage, or search by keyword. |
| 🗣 **Sermons & announcements** | Simple text slides for sermon points and rotating announcements, with speaker notes on the stage display. |
| 🖼 **Media** | Photos and videos as slide backgrounds or as their own slides. |
| 🎨 **Themes** | Set fonts, colors and backgrounds once — apply everywhere or per song/section. |
| 🗓 **Schedule** | Build Sunday's order of service by dragging songs, verses and media into place. Save and reuse. |
| 📋 **Stage display** | A screen for the platform: current words big, next slide dimmed, plus a clock. |
| 📱 **Remote control** | Open a link on any phone/tablet on the church Wi-Fi, enter the PIN, and control the service. |

Everything runs **offline and locally** during a service — if the internet is
down on Sunday, nothing breaks.

## Reporting issues

Found a bug or have an idea? Please
**[open an issue](https://github.com/darljed/project-worshipsavvy/issues/new/choose)** —
choose the **Bug report** or **Feature request** template. Good reports include:

- What you expected vs. what happened
- Steps to reproduce
- Your OS + WorshipSavvy version (Help → About)
- A screenshot or short screen recording if it's visual

For questions and setup help that aren't bugs, use
**[Discussions](https://github.com/darljed/project-worshipsavvy/discussions)**.
